Adaptations for this website:
- Standard glTF 2.0 GLB conversion, with the original skin joints, bind matrices,
  rest transforms and mesh geometry retained except the separate green hair ribbon.
- Unused VRM metadata, facial morph targets, thumbnail and textures removed.
- Same-material primitives combined; textures resized to a maximum of 1024 pixels
  and encoded as palette PNG while retaining their alpha masks.
- Materials converted to nonmetallic, lit PBR surfaces.
- Male upper clothing recolored cream; female clothing recolored rose pink and
  hair chestnut brown. The female green ribbon material/geometry was omitted.
- Humanoid names stored in asset.extras.humanoid and companion bone-map JSON.
