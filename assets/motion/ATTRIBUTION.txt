Motion capture: Carnegie Mellon University, CMU Graphics Lab.
Dataset supported by NSF grant EIA-0196217.
Original dataset and usage conditions: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/

Used captures: subject 07, motions 07_04 and 07_05, both indexed as slow walk.
Original source files: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/07/
BVH conversion: Bruce Hahne / cgspeed, MotionBuilder-friendly release (2010).
Conversion information: https://sites.google.com/a/cgspeed.com/cgspeed/motion-capture/cmu-bvh-conversion
Actual downloaded conversion mirror:
https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh/tree/main/Sequence-001-009/07
https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh/blob/main/READMEFIRST.txt

CMU permits including this motion data in products, including commercial products,
but prohibits directly reselling the data, even in converted form. The BVH
converter places no additional restrictions on the conversion. This website uses
adapted motion curves to animate its anniversary opening, not to sell motion data.

Preparation for this project, 2026-11-07:
- Removed the converter-added first-frame T-pose from animation playback and kept
  it separately as the retargeting reference pose.
- Evaluated BVH forward kinematics, retaining the original joint hierarchy and
  reference offsets. Exported world-space bone quaternions and root-relative
  world-space joint positions in source units.
- Rotated the source coordinate frame by pi about Y to face -Z, and removed only
  the initial horizontal root offset. Absolute root Y remains available.
- Sampled every fourth captured frame (approximately 30 Hz), plus the final frame.
  Original foot/toe positions remain at approximately 120 Hz for contact analysis.
- No timing warp or curve smoothing was applied to these JSON source curves.

Files: 07_04-walk.json and 07_05-walk.json.
Both are continuous walking excerpts; starting and stopping are adapted by the
website and should not be described as separately captured start/stop actions.
